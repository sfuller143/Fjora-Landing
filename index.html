Questions

Good to know.

When can I download Fjora?

Fjora is launching on iOS and Android, starting in Costa Rica this season. Join the waitlist and we'll email you once on launch day. No spam, ever.

Does Fjora track my location?

No. Gathers use a place you choose from search, like a bar, a trailhead or a hostel. Your device's GPS is never used for Gathers, and live location sharing is permanently off.

What's a Companion?

Our version of a friend or follower, except it means something. A Companion is someone you've actually met while traveling. You add each other by username, their check-ins appear in your Explore feed, and you can plan journeys together.

Who can see my journal?

Only you, by default. Every entry has its own "share with Companions" toggle, so sharing is a choice you make each time.

How do Trusted Contacts work? Do they need the app?

They just need a phone number. When you join a Gather, your Trusted Contact gets a text with the place and time. Afterward, Fjora asks if you made it back safe. If you don't answer within the grace period, your contact is texted anyway.

Can I use Fjora outside Costa Rica?

Yes. Your map, check-ins, journal, itineraries, budgets and packing lists work anywhere in the world. Costa Rica is simply where we're kick-starting Gathers and hosted meetups first.

I run a hostel or create travel content. How do I get involved?

See the founding partner section above, or email us. We're working with a limited number of properties and creators this season.
