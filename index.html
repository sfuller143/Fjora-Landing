What makes it different

Not another follower count.

Signals

Send up a real-time flare — "heading to this bar tonight, come say hi" — and meet up with other travelers nearby. No live location tracking, ever.

Your world, mapped

An interactive scratch map of everywhere you've checked in — countries, states, and every trip within them, with photos and ratings for each place.

Private-first journal

Plan itineraries and write freely — nothing is shared until you choose to. Sharing is a decision you make per entry, not a default.

Find things to do

Discover excursions and activities wherever you are, and book straight through to the source — no middleman fees.
